# Dead ends

Approaches tried and dropped, with the numbers. Read before starting perf or layout work. Add a line when you drop one: date, branch, one sentence on what, one on why, where the patch or probe lives. Retry only with a specific reason the shortcoming no longer applies.

- 2026-09-27, annotation-anchor: CSS anchor positioning for the callouts instead of the measuring script. Layout cost 25 to 50% more than main on resize and page turn; only dropping the insets that place the callouts got back to main. An `anchor-name` inside a sheet also stops Blink treating the section as a layout boundary. Patch in the session scratchpad, annotation-anchor/wip-on-4c6b362c.patch.
- 2026-09-27, led-pulse-paint: moving the demo LED pulse to compositor-only properties. It already animates opacity alone and costs 0 style, layout or paint passes in isolation; the audit's LED figure was the dog-ear before #182. Nothing to change.
- 2026-09-27, css-contain: `contain` on the page's small boxes (title blocks, transport deck, sheet content and aside, post-its, callout labels and cards, profile, career, elevation, bill of materials, tech icons, theme picker, folios, open source rows, demo islands and panels). Each one gives its box a paint layer of its own, and at fractional offsets that moves text and lines by a pixel at one width or another (390, 900, 1216, 1300); the few that stay pixel-identical measure nothing. Probes in the session scratchpad, css-contain/probe (ab.mjs, gesture.mjs).
- 2026-09-27, css-contain: `contain: layout size` on each page of a stack (the `article > div` wrappers). Pixel-identical in all four themes at 1440 and 390, with and without script, but a grid item is never a layout boundary in Blink, and resize steps with a demo in view came out 1 to 2% slower in four in-page A/B runs. Adding `paint` clips the turning flap, which is a child of that box. Same probes.
- 2026-09-27, sheet-vars-css: registering `--sheet-inline`/`--sheet-block` with `@property` as lengths, or registering one non-inherited length on the frame and handing it down. Either way the frame resolves new pixels at each resize step and every element under it restyles to inherit them: 50 to 65 ms more style per step without script, against about 6 ms for the unregistered expression. Probe: session scratchpad, sheet-vars-css/quick.mjs, builds dist-var-a (registered) and dist-var-c (non-inherited).
- 2026-09-27, cq-audit: folding the 60 `@container technical-drawing-width` rules into `data-sheet-orientation`/`data-sheet-width` selectors with a media fallback for the no-JS page, and dropping the frame's container. Once the fold lengths stop inheriting the rules cost about 55 ms of a no-JS resize step (117 to 61 ms of layout) and about 10 ms with script at the Marker Editor (51 to 40, one run), nothing at the top, since content-visibility keeps the off-screen stacks out of it. The fallback would move where the no-JS page switches: 40em is 680px of viewport plus a classic scrollbar, 56em is two bands (up to 974px and 1024 to 1037px). Probe in the session scratchpad, cq-audit/probe.mjs, variant nocq-frame.
- 2026-09-27, css-unused: moving index.css rules the first render never uses out to the island sheets or a deferred sheet. After the title-block loop fix, 121 KB of the 416 KB in rules go unused at load (1440, 29%), but every group is a state the reader reaches later (hover and focus 20 KB, no-script 6 KB, page turns, open notes) or a stack off screen, whose style Blink skips under content-visibility. No component's styles are island-only: card.scss serves Panel.astro on the server and CatalogPanel.vue in the island, and #181 already defers the sheets that are. A deferred sheet for later pages would need a script trigger and shows the page unstyled for a frame on the turn. Coverage probes in the session scratchpad, css-unused/probe (cov.mjs, unused.mjs, dead.mjs).
- 2026-09-30, noscript-scroll-gutter: `padding-block-end` or `scrollbar-gutter: stable` on the no-script row to keep its horizontal scrollbar off the sheets. Neither moves it: the scrollbar takes its 6px off the content box before padding counts (a 100px row with 6px of padding still lays the sheet out at 94px in Firefox), and scrollbar-gutter reserves for the block-axis scrollbar only. Auto height on the row does grow it by the scrollbar, but the row is a size container and sizes to nothing, so the sheet's height goes in as `contain-intrinsic-block-size`. Probe in the session scratchpad, noscript-scroll-gutter/bare.mjs.
- 2026-09-28, tech-label-360: keeping `content-visibility: auto` on the Bill of materials by moving it from each chip to the whole list, so the list takes its width from the section and only its height is a guess. Page load layout came out the same with or without it (293 against 288 ms at 360 px, 336 against 325 at 1440, one set of 5 runs), and no single guess fits the list, which is 615 px tall at 360 and 284 at 1440, so it still changes height when it renders. Probe in the session scratchpad, tech-label-360/variants.mjs.
- 2026-09-30, event-dispatch-note: on the Event Dispatch sheet from 688 to 816px, setting the branch note under $res (round 1 of #215), then keeping $res in a right column as wide as the title block with a margin as tall as the block under it. The note alone saved 13px and left the bus on the block. The margin needs the block's height, 78px at 688 and 696, where the ladder stacks the title in two lines, and $res then sat in the band title-fit reads, so the block never got its one-line fit back and the column ran 16 to 49px past the sheet. The branch keeps $res and the tray beside the emitter instead. Probes in the session scratchpad, event-dispatch-note (r2probe.mjs, ink.mjs).
- 2026-09-30, self-hosted-fonts: shipping Google's 2 KB `text=0123456789` kit of Allerta Stencil from the site. Allerta Stencil's OFL reserves the name Allerta and a subset is a modified version, so the site ships the authors' whole TTF as WOFF2 (10.2 KB). Kit and probes in the session scratchpad, self-hosted-fonts.
